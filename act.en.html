Volunteer

A rescued animal travels from the rescue site to the vet, from the vet to a foster home, and to the airport once adopted abroad. Travel with them, or foster, until they find a family.

01

Flight volunteering

Travelling to the US · Canada

Flight volunteers help our rescue dogs take the last step to their forever families and complete the rescue. We are looking for ticketed passengers travelling to the US or Canada on Korean Air, Asiana or Air Canada. Most of our dogs are medium or large, and in Korea, where small dogs are preferred, adoption abroad is often their only chance at a family.

We prepare all the documents for the dog’s travel and cover the costs. All you need to do is come to the airport 30 minutes earlier than planned to meet us and the dogs.

02

Transport in Korea

Car · public transport · domestic flights

Transport volunteers are an essential part of every rescue: rescued dogs need to get from the rescue site to the vet, from the vet to foster homes or our training facility, and to the airport when they are adopted abroad.

Most trips are by car, but you can also help by train, bus or domestic flight. Join WeAct’s KakaoTalk open chat and sign up for the trips you can make.

03

Foster care

Starts after an interview

At a foster home, animals get to live in a home for the first time, surrounded by love and care. Rescued animals learn that not all humans are bad, which is the beginning of their rehabilitation.

That is why foster families play such a crucial role in giving rescued animals a real chance at adoption. To become a WeAct foster, fill out an application; our foster team will then interview you.
