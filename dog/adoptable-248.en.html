Meet Betty

Betty shines her brightest when she smiles! She thoroughly enjoys her playtime in the field with her dog friends. Her gentle manners are evident as she approaches them with a wagging tail, effortlessly getting along even with smaller friends. Once Betty discovers a compatible playmate, she gleefully engages, running and rolling on the ground, savoring every moment of her time in the yard. Betty used to be startled and pull away whenever human hands touched her, but thanks to consistent training and care, she now shows wonderful and positive changes, quietly approaching people and accepting gentle touches without running away.

Before you bring them home

Health

Betty is currently healthy, but given her age, please keep a mindful and steady eye on her overall health management.

Needs help with

Because Betty prefers outdoor potty, consistent potty training will be helpful if she needs indoor potty.

Ideal family

Betty is an independent yet bright and curious friend. She would match best with a family who respects her personal time while gently helping her explore new experiences and build confidence. As she is becoming more comfortable around people but still remains cautious about physical touch, please respect the distance and speed where Betty feels most at ease. If there is a resident dog, a friend with moderate energy and good manners who respects each other space when resting would be ideal. We are waiting for a forever family who will faithfully protect Betty so she can feel completely safe and share her heart in her own special way.

The day Betty was rescuedRescued at · #SiheungDogFarm

In December 2019, the #SiheungDogFarm entered the spotlight as the story of Yumi emerged—a dog that miraculously escaped with extensive burns covering its entire body. Another animal welfare organization initially launched a rescue operation, but on Christmas Day, the owner transferred all the animals to a different dog farm. Although their whereabouts remained elusive, persistent efforts eventually convinced the farm owner to surrender them. However, the time spent in slaughterhouses had inflicted profound trauma on these dogs. Taking charge of the situation, WEACT and volunteers provided ceaseless day and night care, leading to the successful rescue of 65 animals from the #SiheungDogFarm. Due to their profound trauma and injuries inflicted by humans, it necessitated an extended period of time and heightened effort to rehabilitate them. Even four years down the line, WEACT remains steadfast in its mission to facilitate the adoption of dogs from #SiheungDogFarm. #weact_betty was one of the rescues from this very location.

Day to day

With people

Betty tends to be reserved around strangers, often showing signs of anxiety and difficulty focusing. However, with familiar guardians at The Home, she displays courage by climbing on them for treats. Instead of approaching Betty eagerly, it's better to gradually reduce the distance and gently touch her under the chin.

Betty enjoys the company of dogs similar in size or smaller than herself, happily approaching them with a wagging tail. She is a polite dog who gets along wonderfully with everyone. However, she tends to step back when approached by overly pushy or hyper dogs.

Energy
