Meet Al

Would you like to be Al is kind human friend? With his pouty yet coy expression, he pretends not to care while quietly following you around, but the moment you offer a play bow, he transforms into a joyful, playful boy! Al loves running around with compatible friends. Whenever he is with dog pals, his mood goes up and up, and he beams with high energy. While new people and unfamiliar places are always challenges for him, Al is a resilient dog of inner strength who will always pull through if you encourage him and give him time. He is incredibly gentle. Even when he is scared and terrified, he never acts aggressively toward humans, instead showing his discomfort simply by pulling his body back. Approach him not with strictness, but with gentleness, and not with force, but with encouragement. Look forward to seeing Al gather all your support in his heart and finally take a courageous step forward.

Before you bring them home

Health

Al is in good health with no issues.

Needs help with

Al prefers outdoor elimination, though he is comfortable going both indoors and outdoors. His leash manners are quite good, but he still experiences tension in unfamiliar environments and around new people. Please build trust slowly at Al's own speed so he can comfortably enjoy walks with his guardian.

Ideal family

Al has some fear when it comes to people. Since his rescue, he’s been making slow but positive progress in building trust with humans, and we’re hoping to find a family who can patiently wait for him to open up at his own pace. If you can offer a calm, supportive environment and help Al continue to grow through positive experiences, you’ll be the perfect match for him.

It would be even better if you’re willing to learn about and provide the training Al needs, such as leash training and socialization, to help him feel more comfortable. Al enjoys the company of other dogs, so having a dog companion who is good with humans could make a huge difference in helping him adapt and thrive.

Al is a bit more fearful when it comes to new things, so it’s important to avoid rushing him. Take it slow and allow him to be gently exposed to new experiences without overwhelming him. When Al shows signs of fear, offering encouragement and patience will help him gain confidence over time.

Right now, Al feels most comfortable in his kennel, but he’s dreaming of a family who can show him that there’s something even stronger and more secure than his kennel— a family that will be his safe, unbreakable support for life.

The day Al was rescuedRescued at · #HwaseongPaltanDogFarm

In March 2021, WEACT received a distressing report about dogs dying at #HwaseongPaltanDogFarm. Following an emergency meeting, we immediately initiated an urgent rescue mission to Hwaseong. Twelve dogs were confined in cramped cages or tethered with short leashes, lacking access to food or clean water. Instead, they were forced to subsist on a bowl filled with rotten scraps. During the day, these dogs had to endure the cold, with their entire bodies soaked from rain and snow. At night, in pitch-black darkness where they couldn't see a step ahead, they had to fend for themselves, without anyone to rely on. With the assistance of RE:BIRTH and volunteers from across the country, WEACT successfully rescued all twelve dogs, along with three strays. Emerging from a harsh and frigid environment, #weact_Al is now experiencing a warm world for the first time.

The day Al was rescued

In March 2021, WEACT received a distressing report about dogs dying at #HwaseongPaltanDogFarm. Following an emergency meeting, we immediately initiated an urgent rescue mission to Hwaseong. Twelve dogs were confined in cramped cages or tethered with short leashes, lacking access to food or clean water. Instead, they were forced to subsist on a bowl filled with rotten scraps.
