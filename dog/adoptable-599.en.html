Meet Eddie

Meet Eddie, a brilliantly perceptive and clever boy! Eddie is quick witted with fantastic learning capabilities. He reads situations fast and has an exceptional ability to grasp the mood around him. At the same time, he possesses a delicate and charmingly picky side, meaning he may respond cautiously in new people or unfamiliar environments. With a firm set of personal boundaries, Eddie is a smart friend who communicates his feelings clearly. If you pay attention to and respect his honest expressions, he will happily show consideration and shower you with affection.

Before you bring them home

Health

He has been diagnosed with bilateral patellar luxation, so consistent care is necessary to avoid putting strain on his joints.

Needs help with

Eddie has a habit of marking indoors and outdoors once he leaves his living space, so potty training is needed. He may also show protective behaviors regarding his crate or food bowl, or show wariness when hands touch his body. Since he is smart and pays close attention to his guardian, please guide him step by step using positive reinforcement and praise.

Ideal family

Eddie thrives with a guardian who can give him focused attention and is attentive to his emotional cues. He prefers meaningful interaction and shared time over being left alone. Considering his clear dislikes and sensitive nature, a calm and thoughtful household would suit him best.He may adapt to a multi-dog home, but the temperament of the resident dog will be very important. Eddie may not fall in love quickly, but once he does, he loves deeply and wholeheartedly.Smart, affectionate, and full of quiet charm, Eddie is waiting for a forever family who will truly understand, respect, and cherish him.

The day Eddie was rescuedRescued at · #NamyangjuPuppyMillVillage

In December 2021, WEACT uncovered a large-scale illegal puppy breeding facility known as the #Namyangju Breeding Farm Village. More than 280 dogs were found living in extremely poor conditions—confined to raised wire cages, surrounded by their own waste, and left to suffer both physically and emotionally.Many of the dogs were forced to endure repeated pregnancies and long periods of confinement. Their condition revealed the painful reality behind puppies often seen in pet shop windows.Eddie was one of these dogs. He had been tied near the entrance by the breeder to act as a guard. At the time of rescue, Eddie was extremely sensitive to touch and reacted defensively, likely due to trauma from past abuse.With the help of multiple organizations and countless volunteers, WEACT was able to secure full relinquishment of ownership for all the dogs. Eddie and the others were finally given the chance to begin a new life—free from cages and fear.
