Sociability with cats

Meet Anon

First impression: a cool model vibe! In reality: a soft cat with a bowl cut? With a chic gaze Anon keeps the butler’s heart on a roller coaster, but a minute later Anon is sleeping soundly, belly up, limbs stretched out, the clumsy cat. Fall for Anon’s charm ♡ Anon is very curious about and responsive to toys, so when suddenly in a good mood, Anon expresses it by dashing around the house in zoomies. Anon loves toys shaped like a little mouse, kicking them with its front paws as it runs around the house, enjoying it as if playing soccer, which is so cute. Anon concentrates and gets absorbed in toys and treats, so please help Anon release energy through various activities. Anon is not overly active, so after playing hard, Anon settles into a comfortable spot to doze or rest. Is there anyone who wants to see Anon’s cute, goofy charm in person?

Before you bring them home

Health

Anon has recovered well and is living daily life without major problems.

Ideal family

Anon seems likely to adapt more easily and quickly in a multi-cat household that has a cat already settled in, rather than as an only cat. A home with a stable, mature senior cat would be good, as Anon can learn sociability through natural interaction between cats and learn how to connect with people. Also, since Anon is independent and does not depend on its guardian, a family that understands and respects the line Anon wants, rather than excessive skinship, is suitable.

The day Anon was rescuedRescued at · #CarAccidentStrayCat

A cat lay in the middle of the road. It was a dangerous place with cars passing nonstop, but the cat could not move at all and was gasping for breath. Its whole body had already been wrecked by two accidents, but amazingly it was still alive. It did not even have the strength to move, but its eyes were clear. “I… still want to live.” Those eyes were clearly saying so. WeAct rescued the cat right away and rushed to the hospital. Remembering its strong heart that never gave up on life, we gave this cat the name Anon.

Day to day

With people

Anon enjoys skinship with a guardian it has come to trust, but, like a cat, shows discomfort when touched on disliked or sensitive spots. Anon can rest comfortably when spending time with a companion who respects its space and wishes. When outsiders come, Anon is a cautious cat who adapts by observing from a distance rather than interacting actively.

Even in a multi-cat household, Anon adapts quickly to the relationships within the group and reacts steadily to other cats. The day Anon was rescued

A cat lay in the middle of the road. It was a dangerous place with cars passing nonstop, but the cat could not move at all and was gasping for breath. Its whole body had already been wrecked by two accidents, but amazingly it was still alive. It did not even have the strength to move, but its eyes were clear. “I… still want to live.” Those eyes were clearly saying so. WeAct rescued the cat right away and rushed to the hospital. Remembering its strong heart that never gave up on life, we gave this cat the name Anon.
