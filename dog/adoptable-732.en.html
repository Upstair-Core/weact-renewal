Meet SomTom

Meet SomTom, a white cotton candy that seems it would blow away if you puffed on it ☁️🤍

SomTom is timid but has the heart of a curious girl! When something new appears, SomTom carefully approaches and looks around, getting to know it slowly at its own pace until it is familiar. Once SomTom chooses a toilet spot or a resting spot, it keeps using the same place, a tidy personality. SomTom feels most comfortable and lives steadily when it has its own space. SomTom may be startled if you approach or touch suddenly. If you wait so SomTom can smell you and confirm you are there, and approach slowly, SomTom will feel safe and open its heart.

Before you bring them home

Health

SomTom has a lot of eye discharge, so the hair around the eyes needs trimming, and regular eye check-ups may be needed.

Needs help with

SomTom has good basic living habits. SomTom does not have much outside experience, but learns new environments at its own pace, so if you do not rush and wait during outdoor activities, SomTom can adapt well.

Ideal family

SomTom is a dog who spends time well alone, but it would be better if there is plenty of time together. At first, SomTom needs time to adapt to an unfamiliar environment, so please make plenty of time to stay close and wait so SomTom can adapt with peace of mind!

If there is a resident dog, a calm friend would be good. Of course, it is also fine without a resident dog. SomTom has a big wish to be loved by people, so being loved alone will make SomTom very happy.

SomTom is gentle and easygoing, so even a household welcoming a dog for the first time should be able to live together without much difficulty. If you approach unhurriedly and relaxed, SomTom will adapt step by step and grow close to the family. SomTom is older, but has a pure and bright energy like no other. We are waiting for a family who will bloom daily happiness together with sweet love like cotton candy ☁️🤍

The day SomTom was rescuedRescued at · Jeju City Shelter

The “Savers Project” is WeAct’s long-term rescue project: we rescue dogs in danger of euthanasia at municipal shelters and do our best to take responsibility for them until they meet new families. This time WeAct headed to Jeju Island. After rescuing the “Jeju animal hoarder,” the “Jeju illegal dog meat farm” and the “dog without a nose,” we had rescued 18 lives in total. But only 3 days after returning, we headed to Jeju again, to rescue dogs at the shelter who were in danger of euthanasia. At the Jeju City Shelter, which has the highest euthanasia rate in the country, WeAct rescued 5 dogs in total, including an old dog with a tumor, a dog with heartworm, and dogs on the euthanasia list. We gave this dog the name “SomTom” and are preparing to welcome a new family. WeAct’s rescues for precious lives will continue.
