Meet Vincie

Owner of 10kg of kindness, 10kg of smarts and 20kg of charm! Huge and so huge-ly cute, the perfect dog. Meet our Vincie!

With a sturdy build, Vincie has a clear presence anywhere! At first sight you may say, “Wow, she’s big!” But after meeting her for a little while, you quickly find out that this big body is filled with love and charm ♡ Unlike her sturdy looks, Vincie has the heart of a girl and is not shy about honestly expressing what she likes. She enjoys fun moments more cheerfully than anyone, yet in daily life she also has the ease to stay calmly by your side and spend comfortable time. Above all, Vincie’s greatest charm is that she holds as much love as her big body! Reassuring just to look at and full of charm once you know her, she is a lovable girl you will want to keep by your side once you meet her. Kindness, smarts and charm, all extra large! Once you fall for the huge-ly cute Vincie, it will be hard to climb back out.

Before you bring them home

Health

Vincie is very healthy right now.

Needs help with

Vincie does not yet fully recognize where to go to the toilet, so she needs toilet training. If you praise her generously and teach her repeatedly, she can form good toilet habits. Please also help her, through consistent walk training, to naturally get used to outside noises and different environments, and to walk safely in step with her guardian and build happy walking habits.

Ideal family

We are waiting for a family who will look beyond the prejudice against big dogs and see the true Vincie, a pure girl with a heart full of love.

Unlike her big body, Vincie has an extremely gentle and soft heart. She is a lovable, girl-like friend who loves being charming and loved more than anyone. A kind family who understands Vincie’s heart and cherishes her lovableness for a long time would be ideal. The type of housing is not very important. Whether an apartment, a villa or a house, it is enough if she can live stably with her family and be loved. If there is a resident dog, a friend of similar size would be even better so they can comfortably accept Vincie’s sturdy build. Also, since Vincie is a large dog, we hope for a guardian who fully understands her strength and size and can lead her steadily. If you trust dignified Vincie while guiding her reliably when needed, you will be able to become a more comfortable and happy family together.

The day Vincie was rescuedRescued at · Suncheon dog slaughterhouse

We arrived in a neighborhood in Suncheon after receiving a tip. The navigation led us not to a remote mountain but to a new town. Right next to people’s peaceful daily lives, very close by, cruel slaughter was taking place. The slaughterer was calling his pet dogs by name one by one and carefully handing out treats. The pet dogs even wore name tags with a phone number on their collars so they would not be mistaken for strays. Slaughterers often keep some dogs separately as “companions.” They call them family and say they could never give them up. But that does not change the fact that this person slaughters living dogs. With the same hands that loved his pet dogs, he took the lives of other dogs far too easily. WeAct moved with one purpose, to stop the slaughter, and by the time we arrived everything had already been cleared away. The city hall, which had been dispatched a few days earlier after a report, had quickly moved the dogs in danger of slaughter to the municipal shelter. Thanks to that, the dogs were able to survive. Unfortunately, however, the slaughterhouse owner mobilized people to hurriedly tear the slaughterhouse down, and most of the evidence was lost. WeAct rescued seven dogs in total: six that had been moved to the municipal shelter and one dog that the slaughterer had kept. There is no reason anywhere for lives that could become family to be consumed as meat. We gave this dog the name “Vincie,” not “a dog from a slaughterhouse,” and will do our best to help Vincie find a comfortable life in a family’s embrace.
