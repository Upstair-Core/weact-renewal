Meet Reim

With her soft, creamy white face, little yellow freckles, and adorable rounded ears that make her look like a teddy bear, meet Reim, the very definition of cuteness!

Spend a little time watching Reim, and you'll quickly discover what a delightful and entertaining girl she is. Whenever something exciting happens, she is one of the first to come running over. And when she spots a toy she likes, her eyes sparkle like the Milky Way in the night sky! Reim especially loves sharing fun experiences with others. Although she is perfectly capable of entertaining herself, she gets even more excited when she has someone to play with. She has a naturally outgoing and sociable personality! Even when she's having the time of her life playing, she can quickly pick up new things when someone teaches her. She is a clever and attentive girl who can learn basic commands such as "sit" and "paw" in a surprisingly short amount of time.

Reim is the kind of a smart girl who plays with endless enthusiasm but knows how to focus when it's time to learn. We thought she was already adorable enough, but it turns out she has brains to match her beauty!

Before you bring them home

Health

Reim is currently undergoing treatment for heartworm. She is taking post-treatment medication and is completing the final stage of her treatment.

Until treatment is fully completed, her activity must be restricted, and strenuous exercise should be avoided. She will need careful monitoring and a calm routine during this recovery period.

Needs help with

Reim is currently learning how to manage her excitement around outside stimuli and walk safely on a leash. When she encounters exciting sights and sounds, she can become overstimulated and pull on the leash. Consistent practice using treats and verbal commands will help her focus on her training and learn to walk calmly.

Reim understands the use of indoor pee pads for urination, although she occasionally has accidents or marks her territory depending on the environment. She currently tends to poop outdoors. Continued and consistent potty training will help her maintain reliable bathroom habits as she adjusts to a new home and environment.

Ideal family

We are looking for a loving family who will enjoy going on walks, running around, and creating countless happy memories with our smiley girl, Reim! Since she is a high-energy dog, she will need plenty of daily walks and fun playtime. It would also be wonderful to find a family committed to helping her learn to stay calm and walk safely on a leash, especially when she gets excited by outside stimuli. Reim absolutely loves playing and running around with other dogs, so having a canine companion at home who enjoys playing with her could make her daily life even more enriching and fun.

Of course, she would also be perfectly happy as the only dog in the household, soaking up all the love and attention from her family! Reim is waiting for a family who will embrace her lively spirit, share in her enthusiasm for life, laugh together, and continue growing alongside her every day.

The day Reim was rescuedRescued at · #JeonNamIllegalDogFarm

More than two years have passed since the ban on the dog meat industry was officially enacted. The government established a three-year grace period, during which those involved in the industry could prepare to transition to other occupations or close their businesses without facing penalties. The summer of 2026 marks the final summer of this grace period. Because it is the last summer in which people can still make money from slaughtering dogs, countless dogs were still losing their lives at dog farms.

At a dog farm in Gangjin, South Jeolla Province, the dogs who had been there when the initial report was received had disappeared, and their places had already been filled by new dogs. To some people, they may have been nothing more than a bowl of dog meat soup. But the dogs living there were innocent, gentle souls who, despite their frightened expressions, would wag their tails whenever a person approached. Reim was one of them.

While the other dogs were confined in raised wire cages, Reim was protecting her newborn puppies on muddy ground where her paws sank deep into the mud. She was a devoted mother who endured these harsh conditions while caring for her little ones, despite having no access to proper drinking water and being forced to survive on food waste. Even after everything she had endured, the first thing Reim showed when someone approached was not fear or suspicion, but a welcoming wag of her tail.

Determined to put an end to this cruel cycle, WEACT decided to rescue the dogs. At last, Reim and her puppies were able to leave the dog farm behind.

A life that could have ended as someone's meal is now preparing to begin a new chapter as a beloved companion animal.

Reim worked harder than anyone to protect her puppies. Now, it is her turn to stop being just a mother dog and finally experience life as a cherished family companion, surrounded by love.
