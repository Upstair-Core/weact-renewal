Meet Jjangi

Loves people! Sticking its bottom right next to you and radiating trust, the charming Jjangi! Meet Jjangi -(❁´◡`❁)

Jjangi is a lovable dog whose small charms keep appearing the longer you live together. Indoors, Jjangi plays with toys and moves around actively, and outside, Jjangi calmly looks around and enjoys the outing. Cheerful at home! Dignified on walks! Jjangi shows a surprising contrast.

For Jjangi, who loves to eat, the smell of food is the best perfume in the world. When Jjangi smells food, it sniffs, its eyes light up, and it gazes at you with hopeful eyes, showing its sweetheart side. Isn’t it so cute that Jjangi cannot hide its feelings in front of what it loves?! Meeting Jjangi’s many charms in ordinary daily life always makes us smile.

Before you bring them home

Health

Jjangi is currently being treated for heartworm. On long car rides, Jjangi may show mild motion sickness such as drooling.

Needs help with

Jjangi lived in an environment that lacked care, so Jjangi needs time to slowly learn the rules of life in a new home. Jjangi may show anxiety when alone or in an enclosed space, so please help Jjangi practice resting alone within a stable daily routine.

Please help improve frequent marking with consistent toilet training and short, frequent walks, and also watch Jjangi’s eating speed, as Jjangi tends to eat too fast.

Ideal family

We hope Jjangi can live as an only dog with a family who can give plenty of attention and love.

Thinking of the time Jjangi did not receive enough care, Jjangi’s way of reaching out to share affection beside people feels all the more precious. For Jjangi, time sitting beside you, stroking its body and making eye contact, brings great joy.

Please spend time not only walking and playing but also sitting beside Jjangi and giving it lots of love. And please respect Jjangi’s pace while it adjusts to a new life and learns the habits it needs. Jjangi acts spoiled out of a wish to spend lots of time with family, but also has a dignified side, so Jjangi can learn plenty of training well!

Jjangi shows affection in every small moment of daily life. We are waiting for a family who will answer that love and give Jjangi a loved daily life -(❁´◡`❁)

The day Jjangi was rescuedRescued at · Neglected dog in Dangjin

“I paid several million won for this dog, but circumstances came up, so I left it at my parents’ house in the countryside.” It may be a story you have heard before. It is repeated so often that we may have grown numb to the fact that this can mean abandoning and neglecting a life. The dogs WeAct met in Dangjin were in exactly this situation. What we found when we arrived was devastating. In a barn so neglected that mold was growing on the feces, two Bichon Frises had been left, and in the yard a Pomeranian was left alone. The eldest daughter had left the two Bichons with parents who had trouble moving around, and the second daughter had left the Pomeranian behind because it “barks.” Left in the care of the family this way, the dogs had been surviving day by day on food waste, in mold and stench. While people buy so easily and pass the burden on so easily, the price of those choices has always been paid by the most powerless lives. So that Jjangi's life is no longer decided by someone else’s circumstances, Jjangi is starting a new life in a safe place.

Day to day

With people

Jjangi wags its tail and approaches even people it has just met, and accepts touch comfortably! Jjangi has no shyness and loves being petted anywhere on its body. When the petting hand stops, Jjangi may show its feelings by asking for a little more.

With other dogs

Jjangi’s reaction tends to differ depending on the other dog’s size. When a bigger dog approaches, Jjangi may get scared and hide, or show that it would rather the dog not come close. However, the expression can become strong, so please stay beside Jjangi and watch, and manage the distance from other dogs.

Energy

Jjangi loves going on walks. Outside, Jjangi calmly enjoys the outing, and indoors shows its energy by playing with toys or running around. Please enjoy two walks of about 30 minutes a day together with toy play at home! Time spent walking and playing with the family makes Jjangi’s days richer -(❁´◡`❁)
