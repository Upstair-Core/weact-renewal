Meet Dangdang

Dangdang makes a confident entrance! Meet Dangdang, who wins hearts fair and square with cuteness!

Dangdang shows off a confident charm, just like the name! Cute even when sitting still, but once you fall for Dangdang there is no climbing back out ♡ When Dangdang wants something, it stares with big, shining eyes and stamps its paws! Asking for exactly what it wants with its whole body, even that is lovable. With such a pleading look, how could you pretend not to notice? And when you squish and rub Dangdang, it goes completely limp and leaves itself in your hands, so sweet-natured! Behind the confident name and confident walk hides a soft, squishy charm. Confident on the outside, squishy to the touch! The more you know Dangdang, the more you fall for it. Isn’t this charm simply unfair? ♡

Before you bring them home

Health

Dangdang is healthy right now! However, Dangdang has a lot of tear staining, so please keep the area around the eyes clean and help with consistent tear care.

Needs help with

Dangdang does not have much walking experience yet, so please teach walking step by step so that Dangdang can get to know the outside world safely and happily. ♡ Dangdang is also still learning to use pee pads, so there may be accidents. Please praise Dangdang generously when it goes on the pad, and wait patiently so Dangdang can slowly learn toilet habits in the new home.

Ideal family

No more time when life was decided by someone else’s circumstances and Dangdang had to endure alone! From now on, Dangdang is ready to move toward its own happiness, confidently, just like its name! ♡ Please be a kind family who will not rush, even if Dangdang is still clumsy at some things, and will teach step by step. We hope you will spend plenty of time with Dangdang and guide it warmly so it can learn, one by one, the habits needed in daily life such as walks and toileting. If there is a resident dog, a calm friend of similar size would suit Dangdang best, as it will feel comfortable. Of course, life as an only dog, where Dangdang can fully share the family’s love, is also good. ♡ Dangdang, who charms with sparkling eyes and follows anyone who adores it. ♡ We hope Dangdang will find not a place to stay for a while and leave, but a safe and warm family embrace to come home to. Dangdang will fill its days with love, not with someone else’s circumstances, and be happy to the fullest at its family’s side. We are waiting for a family who will join that new beginning and be a reliable ally for a long time.

The day Dangdang was rescuedRescued at · Neglected dog in Dangjin

“I paid several million won for this dog, but circumstances came up, so I left it at my parents’ house in the countryside.” It may be a story you have heard before. It is repeated so often that we may have grown numb to the fact that this can mean abandoning and neglecting a life. The dogs WeAct met in Dangjin were in exactly this situation. What we found when we arrived was devastating. In a barn so neglected that mold was growing on the feces, two Bichon Frises had been left, and in the yard a Pomeranian was left alone. The eldest daughter had left the two Bichons with parents who had trouble moving around, and the second daughter had left the Pomeranian behind because it “barks.” Left in the care of the family this way, the dogs had been surviving day by day on food waste, in mold and stench. While people buy so easily and pass the burden on so easily, the price of those choices has always been paid by the most powerless lives. So that Dangdang's life is no longer decided by someone else’s circumstances, Dangdang is starting a new life in a safe place.
