The day Charcoal was rescuedRescued at · Seongnam illegal dog meat farm

Back at the illegal dog meat farm in Seongnam. This is no unfamiliar site for WeAct. We had rescued many animals here over several rescues. After each rescue we hoped there would be no more victims, but sadly that hope was crushed again. The farm we revisited had been filled with new lives. Two rabbits were hiding on a shelf deep inside a greenhouse where people’s eyes rarely reach, and in another elevated wire cage, two young puppies were locked up. A place where countless lives had already been sacrificed, a slaughterhouse with no end in sight. Through earlier rescues many animals left there, but the places that should have been empty were being filled with other animals. The rescue we thought was over had to begin again, and WeAct once again rescued animals from that place.

Characteristics

Charcoal is a black rabbit! Black rabbits are said to be usually timid. But for a timid rabbit, Charcoal is overflowing with curiosity! Charcoal likes hideout houses and knows how to have quiet time alone. Charcoal runs away, but even while struggling, accepts being held and having nails trimmed well.
