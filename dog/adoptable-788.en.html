PeopleNooni enjoys interacting with people, but she tends to be a little cautious when meeting someone for the first time.

Nooni enjoys interacting with people, but she tends to be a little cautious when meeting someone for the first time. When unfamiliar people approach her, she may keep her distance at the beginning, observing them far away or even pretending not to be interested. Once she's had enough time to explore and feels safe, she'll cautiously approach to say hello. Rather than approaching her too enthusiastically right away, please give Nooni the opportunity to come to you on her own.

Once she opens her heart to someone, she transforms into an affectionate little sweetheart! She'll approach for kisses, happily relax her whole body, and enjoy being petted. When you stop, she may look at you longingly or nudge her head toward your hand, asking for just a little more affection.

However, she isn't comfortable with being suddenly picked up or handled forcefully. When something makes her uncomfortable, she may gently lift one of her front paws as if to say, "Wait a minute!" Respecting these little signals will help her feel safe and open up even more. The secret to winning Nooni’s heart is simply patience and gentle affection.

Other dogsJust as she can be a little shy when unfamiliar people approach her, Nooni is also thoughtful and cautious when meeting new canine friends.

Just as she can be a little shy when unfamiliar people approach her, Nooni is also thoughtful and cautious when meeting new canine friends. She genuinely enjoys spending time with other dogs and is quite sociable, but she may need a little time before feeling comfortable with new friends. When meeting unfamiliar dogs, she may initially be wary, growl, or raise her hackles. But once she realizes they are safe, she becomes a wonderful companion who enjoys relying on and learning from her friends.

One of her favorite activities with dogs she knows well is playful mouth-wrestling, where they open their mouths and gently play-bite each other like little crocodiles. Watching them playfully tussle and then happily continue playing together is simply adorable!

EnergyNooni may be small, but she's packed with exciting energy!

Nooni may be small, but she's packed with exciting energy! She's a big fan of walks and has wonderful outdoor manners, even comfortably doing her potty outdoors. Exploring the world and sniffing all the interesting new smells are some of her favorite activities. Please provide at least two walks a day so she has plenty of opportunities to burn off her energy. But the fun doesn't stop when she gets home! Nooni loves bringing her toys over and asking you to throw them, or happily running around and playing by herself.

Playing fetch is one of her absolute favorite activities, so please make time for even a few short play sessions throughout the day. Sharing the activities she loves will also help strengthen the bond between Nooni and her family.

Meet Nooni

Meet Nooni, an adorable girl whose charm goes far beyond her signature pink-speckled nose! Nooni is a natural-born fetch enthusiast! She loves bringing toys over for people to throw, chasing after them, and even entertaining herself with solo play. Be prepared for plenty of adorable toy deliveries throughout the day! Though initially a little shy, Nooni has a sweet and affectionate side once she feels comfortable. She'll sneak over for kisses, nudge your hand for pets, and even squeeze her head in when someone else is getting attention. She also has a funny, independent streak, sometimes pretending to enjoy a treat before discreetly spitting it out if it's not to her liking!

With her playful personality, clever antics, and loving heart, Nooni is sure to bring endless smiles and laughter to her forever family.

Before you bring them home

Health

Nooni is currently healthy.

Needs help with

Nooni is still learning about the world around her and will need time to adjust to new environments. She may feel frightened by sudden physical contact from unfamiliar people, being chased, or loud and overly energetic movements. Rather than forcing interactions, give her opportunities to explore and make her own choices at her own pace.

She also responds well to being called by name in a gentle, high-pitched voice and receiving praise, which is a wonderful way to build a positive connection with her.

When she's bored, she may occasionally entertain herself by chewing or tearing up blankets and cushions. Please help her release her energy through sufficient walks and playtime, and provide appropriate chew toys to keep her happily occupied.

With positive experiences, patience, and plenty of encouragement as she learns new things, Nooni will continue growing into an even more wonderful companion!

Ideal family

Nooni is looking for a loving family who will respect her pace, enjoy daily walks and playtime, and happily throw her favorite toys!

She may be shy at first, so she'll need a family who gives her time to feel safe and approach on her own. With patience and understanding, her trust will blossom into sweet kisses, affectionate nudges, and excited toy deliveries.

An active family who can provide plenty of exercise and play would be ideal. If there's another dog in the home, a calm and friendly companion could help her settle in.

Nooni may have a few playful quirks, but her unique personality and loving heart make her truly special. She's waiting for a family who will embrace all her little quirks and cherish her for a lifetime.

The day Nooni was rescuedRescued at · #HwaseongIllegalSlaughterHouse

The eyes of the 11 dogs we first encountered on the night of April 2 at an illegal slaughterhouse in Hwaseong were hard to forget. Alongside a busy road filled with passing cars, the cries of these vulnerable animals echoed through the night.

Despite the owner's claims that the dogs were being fed dog food, their bowls were filled with food waste. We were told they could drink water from a nearby stream, but the dogs were confined to short chains and cramped elevated wire cages, unable to even reach the water on their own. It had been only 42 days since these dogs were given the chance to smile again, yet the facility had already been filled with another group of dogs. Why must these vulnerable lives continue to flow into places like this, only to suffer the same fate? With the cooperation of local authorities, WEACT successfully rescued the dogs and secured a commitment to dismantle the facility to prevent this tragedy from happening again.
