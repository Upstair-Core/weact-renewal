Meet Mars

A cappuccino pattern where beige and white blend softly, and a wronged-looking expression on a lovable face! Meet Mars, who gets cuter the closer you look.♥︎ He likes people, he likes dog friends, and above all, he is a puppy who loves to play!

And treats, of course! Even Mars, who has so many things he likes, sometimes has a moment of worry. He comes running when called, but right in front of the person he hesitates, 'Should I take one more step, or not?' Meet Mars, whose charm even includes the way he scurries over and wonders about that last step!

Before you bring them home

Health

Mars is healthy!

Needs help with

Mars is learning about walks and indoor toileting. Once he gets used to his harness and leash, please start with short walks. At the center he tends to relieve himself in a corner of the kennel, so please teach him a set toilet spot at home step by step and praise him when he finds it. In a car he gets mildly carsick or sometimes pees and poops. Please help him get used to it slowly, starting with short trips.

Ideal family

A family who understands a puppy's overflowing energy and can run and play with Mars every day would suit him well. We hope for a family who will play with toys to the fullest, gradually widen his walking routes, and praise him lavishly on the days he finds his toilet spot. He has a bright personality that goes up to both people and friends first, and adapts quickly, so we look forward to seeing Mars build new experiences one by one with his family.

He would be happy as an only dog, showered with his family's love, and if there is a dog in the home whose play style matches his, he will have a friend to run around with every day and be even happier. We are waiting for a forever family who will smile and reach out a hand to Mars, who scurries over when called and wonders about that last step.♥︎

The day Mars was rescuedRescued at · Pyeongtaek Animal Shelter, Savers Project

WeAct's "Savers Project" gives shelter animals at risk of euthanasia a chance at a new life, and rescues another animal into each place freed up by an adoption, creating a virtuous cycle of rescue.

Saving a life is not the same as living fully. Rescue is only truly complete when the dogs are embraced by a family who can make them smile with happiness, which is why WeAct will never stop.

Through this Savers Project, WeAct rescued eight dogs in total from the Pyeongtaek Animal Shelter. From a dog abandoned by a new-style pet shop and then abandoned again by another, to a dog with deep wounds where wire wrapped around the body had exposed the skin, eight lives, each carrying a painful story, have come out into the world once again.

We hope this rescue is not only a way out of the shelter but the beginning of hope that each of them can become the most precious family in the world to someone. WeAct will stay with them to the end, so that the remaining days of these eight lives are filled with love rather than waiting, and with happiness rather than fear.
