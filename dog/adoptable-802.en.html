Meet Weepoong

Meet Weepoong, who patters over on cute little steps when you call his name, "Weepoong~"♥︎

Seeing him come running happily with his round face, you want to call his name one more time.

He loves people so much that he greets even people he meets for the first time, and welcomes a petting hand gladly. He is a sweetheart who shows his affection for people without holding back.

Weepoong's tastes are clear in his toys too. He gets excited about rolling balls, squeaky toys and toys he can chew on. Tasty meals and treats are also among Weepoong's joys!

Before you bring them home

Health

Weepoong finished heartworm treatment in October 2026 and is healthy now!

Needs help with

Weepoong knows what a pee pad is, but he sometimes marks indoors.

Please teach him where to go to the toilet step by step, and praise him right away when he uses the pad well. If you guide him to his house with a treat or kibble, he can go in and rest comfortably. At the center, when people leave, he whimpers briefly but soon settles down. If you practice resting alone together, starting with short periods, he will be able to rest comfortably in a place of his own.♥︎

Ideal family

Weepoong needs a family who will warmly receive his joy at seeing people and teach him how to live together, one step at a time. Please greet Weepoong with a smile when he patters over as you call, and pet him lovingly when he comes to your side. For Weepoong, who loves people so much, such time of meeting eyes and sharing hearts will be a great joy of his day.

Please also listen to Weepoong in the moments he is afraid. So that he is not startled by a hand moving over his head, it helps to extend your hand slowly from a side where he can see it. As he learns daily life one thing at a time, with a family who celebrates small successes together and waits one more time during clumsy moments, Weepoong will gradually feel more at ease.

He would be happy as an only dog, showered with love, or with a dog in the home who can play and rest at a similar pace. We hope Weepoong's favorite things fill the ordinary daily life he shares with his family. We are waiting for a forever family who will call his name in a kind voice for a long, long time to Weepoong, who runs over happily whenever he is called.♥︎

The day Weepoong was rescuedRescued at · Dangjin neglected dog

"He's a dog I bought for several million won, but because of my circumstances I left him at my parents' house in the countryside."

It may be a story you have heard once or twice. It is repeated so often that we may have grown numb even to the fact that it can mean abandoning and neglecting a life. The dogs WeAct met in Dangjin were like that too. What we found on arrival was heartbreaking. In a barn so neglected that mold grew on the droppings were two Bichons, and in the yard a Pomeranian had been left alone. The eldest daughter had left the two Bichons with her parents, who have difficulty moving around, and the second daughter had left the Pomeranian behind because it 'barks.' The dogs, left in the hands of family, were getting through each day on food waste, in mold and stench. While people buy too easily and pass the burden on too easily, the price of those choices has always been paid by the most powerless lives. Now Weepoong begins a new life in a safe place, so that his life is no longer decided by someone else's circumstances.
