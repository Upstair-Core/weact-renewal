When he first meets a friend with a very large size difference, please help him look the other dog over from a comfortable distance and get used to it slowly.

Meet Baezzi

Baezzi's deadly proportions shine even more when he comes running. Call "Baezzi!" and wherever he is, he lifts his head and hurries over on his short legs. The force of his wagging tail sets his bottom wiggling too, and his face is full of a bright smile! Watching him race toward you on those short legs, busier than anyone, you find yourself smiling along with Baezzi.♥︎

Baezzi cannot hide his feelings in front of people he likes. He is thrilled to play together, but he enjoys the time of being quietly petted by your side just as much. When you pull your hand away after petting him for a good while, Baezzi lets you know he still has a little petting time left, such a sweetheart!

He plays happily with just one ball, and when playtime is over he settles into a soft spot. Even while relaxing like that, when he hears his name his short legs get busy again. That is why calling Baezzi's name keeps making people happy.♥︎

Before you bring them home

Health

Baezzi is healthy!

Needs help with

Baezzi sometimes barks briefly while waiting for his meal. Give him his food when he has quieted down for a moment, to help him learn to wait calmly at mealtimes. He gets carsick in a car. Please start with short trips to nearby places and help him get used to traveling by car slowly.

Ideal family

A family who steadily takes care of walks and outdoor toilet time, and plays with him at home whenever they can, would suit Baezzi well. He showed that he adapts well to unfamiliar environments, playing happily with friends and sharing treats at a cafe he was visiting for the first time. Whether in an apartment or a house, please create a daily life where you can get some fresh air together and come back to rest comfortably on a soft spot. At the first meeting, please give Baezzi time to say hello first, and when he comes over to show his affection, we hope you will share in that joy.♥︎

The day Baezzi was rescuedRescued at · Dog trapped in concrete

After rescuing Huimang, who had been trapped in concrete, a tip came in that the same owner kept more dogs. At about 2 p.m. on a summer afternoon during a spell of heat, WeAct activists arrived at a remote forest in Hwacheon where there was hardly any phone signal. After pushing through grass far taller than a person and checking boxes and pots one by one for about three hours, they found dogs and rabbits left neglected there. The rabbits were shut in narrow wire cages where they could barely move, and the dogs wore rusty collars, with thick iron chains about 1 m long attached to them and fixed firmly to the ground and to trees. The food bowls were full of insects, and one dog was gnawing on the carcass of a chicken. The dogs, cut off from the outside world for a long time, were very wary as the activists approached. After persuading the owner on site for a long time, the activists got him to relinquish ownership. By the time they had rescued five dogs and two rabbits and finished the work at the site, it was around 3 to 4 a.m. the next morning, well over 12 hours after arriving at the forest. One of the dogs they met in the forest that day is Baezzi. Now Baezzi has left the grass behind and is waiting for a happy everyday life with a family.
