Meet Teunteun

Teunteun has a strong will to live, and he has bravely and patiently endured his hard treatment.

Even while calmly enduring difficult treatment and procedures, he wags his tail gently at people, and you can feel the strength in him that never gives way, whatever the moment. Yet beneath that dignified manner there is also plenty of the innocent little prince. When a tasty treat appears in front of him, his eyes light up in delight, but when it's time for medicine, he suddenly turns his head away as if nothing had happened. The way he shows so clearly what he likes and dislikes makes us laugh.

Before you bring them home

Health

Teunteun has osteosarcoma, which has weakened his bones, and he needs continued follow-up. He currently cannot stand or move around freely on his own, and needs plenty of rest and stability. For osteosarcoma treatment and fracture recovery, he needs regular hospital visits, follow-up checks and ongoing medication. Because his bones are weak, please avoid strenuous activity or impact and look after him with great care.

Needs help with

What Teunteun needs most right now is not training but an environment where he can focus on treatment and recovery. For now, it is enough for him to rest comfortably day by day and regain his health, without having to do anything well.

Ideal family

Teunteun spent a long time alone, without receiving proper warm human touch. After being rescued in a painful condition and enduring hard treatment, we hope he will never have to feel lonely again. We are waiting for a family who will stay by Teunteun's side to the end, through regular hospital visits, medication and careful care. He doesn't need long walks or anything special. An ordinary day is enough: napping together on a warm blanket, sharing tasty treats, calling his name and petting him gently. Please fill the loneliness he has endured alone for so long, one by one, with warm memories made together as a family.

We don't know how much time he has left, but please be Teunteun's first and last warm home, so that for that time he can live as the most loved dog in the world.

The day Teunteun was rescuedRescued at · Retriever found in the water

An urgent tip came in to WeAct asking us to rescue a Retriever stuck in the water. When the person who reported him first saw him, they thought he was just enjoying a swim. But as time passed, he never left the spot. Seeing him stay in the same place for a full eight hours since morning, they finally realized how serious the situation was. He wasn't choosing to stay in the water. He couldn't get out.

He had braced his whole body to keep from being swept away by the strong current. After the report, he was rescued from the waterway and taken to the Pyeongtaek Animal Protection Center, but he was in critical condition, unable to stand or even hold his head up. WeAct confirmed how urgent his state was and headed straight to the shelter. With the cooperation of the Pyeongtaek Animal Protection Center, we took him over quickly and carefully carried him to the hospital while he received IV fluids. We don't know how he ended up in the water, or why he had to hold on alone for eight hours. Yet even in his exhausted body, he wagged his tail at us and carefully lifted his head to greet us. Seeing that small sign, we gave him the name Teunteun, in the hope that he would pull through and regain his health. Please stay by Teunteun's side, so that he never has to hold on alone again.

Day to day

With people

Even during difficult treatment, when people come close, he opens up calmly instead of staying on guard. If you reach out a hand gently, he quietly accepts the touch, and he seems reassured just by having someone with him, kindly making room beside him. Even when his body hurts, he wags his tail at people, and you can feel how glad he is of anyone's warm attention.

With other dogs

For now, he is resting comfortably in a separate space away from the other dogs so that he can focus fully on recovering. Still, he is a gentlemanly dog who gets along peacefully with both small and large friends, without any trouble.

Energy

Teunteun is focused on recovery and rest at the moment, and he spends most of the day lying down comfortably. He sleeps for much of the time, and when he is awake he lies there quietly sharing time with people who pet him. Getting plenty of rest is the most important thing for Teunteun right now.
