The day Siri was rescuedRescued at · Pyeongtaek Animal Shelter, Savers Project

WeAct's "Savers Project" gives shelter animals at risk of euthanasia a chance at a new life, and rescues another animal into each place freed up by an adoption, creating a virtuous cycle of rescue.

Saving a life is not the same as living fully. Rescue is only truly complete when the dogs are embraced by a family who can make them smile with happiness, which is why WeAct will never stop.

Through this Savers Project, WeAct rescued eight dogs in total from the Pyeongtaek Animal Shelter. From a dog abandoned by a new-style pet shop and then abandoned again by another, to a dog with deep wounds where wire wrapped around the body had exposed the skin, eight lives, each carrying a painful story, have come out into the world once again.

We hope this rescue is not only a way out of the shelter but the beginning of hope that each of them can become the most precious family in the world to someone. WeAct will stay with them to the end, so that the remaining days of these eight lives are filled with love rather than waiting, and with happiness rather than fear.
