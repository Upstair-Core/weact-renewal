The day Dottie was rescuedRescued at · Pet shop rescue dog

WeAct heard that a new-style pet shop was operating and went to the site. It was made to look like a dog cafe, but the dogs were kept in a cramped space and constantly exposed to the touch of countless people. The operator said the dogs were 'healthy and well cared for.' They also said sick dogs were taken to the vet and came back confirmed healthy. But the dogs we saw with our own eyes told a different story. Some were scratching themselves or had lost fur, and many coughed repeatedly. Tests confirmed infections with various diseases, and the dogs suspected of skin and respiratory conditions needed further testing. The dogs who were said to be 'healthy and well cared for' were in fact being treated as merchandise in a cramped space, without proper care for their illnesses, and kept exposed to unwanted touching. We could not leave them there any longer. WeAct kept up its persuasion for a long time and finally obtained a written statement relinquishing ownership of 21 dogs. One of the dogs rescued there is Dottie. Now Dottie is no longer a product, and is getting ready to live an ordinary daily life, safely protected in the arms of a warm family.
