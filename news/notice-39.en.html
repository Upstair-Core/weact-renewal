2023, THANK YOU FOR ACTING WITH US!

Jena, abandoned near the Namyangju puppy mill rescue site and rescued

An eventful year has come to a close. It began with the rescue of dead and missing dogs from an abandoned puppy mill in Namyangju, followed by the rescue of the 'puppy mill hell village' in the Namyangju area, uncovered one site after another. The horrific reality of puppy mills began to come out into the world. Then, at a licensed puppy mill in Hwaseong that shocked everyone, an enormous 1,426 dogs were rescued through the solidarity of animal rescue groups.

Bbo, Kari and Sweet, puppies of Doa, who was rescued pregnant from the licensed Hwaseong puppy mill

Abo, Kado, Nacho and Molly, puppies of Beurin, who was rescued pregnant from the licensed Hwaseong puppy mill

The scene of the 'Bye Buy' adoption campaign

As the reality of puppy mills hidden behind glamorous pet shops became known, many people raised their voices with WeAct about the truth of pet shop consumption. To spread those voices further, WeAct hosted the Bye Buy adoption campaign last November, and with the help of many people we were able to finish the campaign successfully.

A WeAct child activist at the rally to end dog meat

In 2023, animal abuse in South Korea was not happening only in puppy mills. Korea has 15 million pet owners, yet every year hundreds of thousands of dogs lose their lives as 'meat'. Thanks to citizens and animal groups who kept raising their voices together, the special act to end dog meat finally passed the plenary session of the National Assembly.

Taeyang, rescued after being used as a prop in the dog meat association's protest

Jayu, rescued after being used as a prop in the dog meat association's protest

In backlash against this movement, the 'dog meat association' held a rally last November and abandoned dogs on the roadside, locked in cramped mesh cages. Once again animal groups joined in solidarity and rescued 11 dogs in all. That is how Taeyang and Jayu came into WeAct's arms.

Playful Taeyang marking his first Christmas at The Home

Nacho taking his adoption-notice photos, cheered on by many 'aunties'

2023 was a year hot with the terrible truth hidden in pet shops and Korea's dog meat problem. The reason we could run more passionately than ever is you. We got this far because you raised your voices with us, gave your precious donations for the animals, and held our hands without looking away even in the moments we wanted to give up.

Thank you sincerely for ACTing with us in 2023.

We will keep on acting.

2023, THANK YOU FOR ACTING WITH US!
