Rescue news · 2026.04.19

Tumor-ridden white dog neglected in Gimhae

Please donate for Gijeok's miracle.

The time of neglect became lumps, ruining the white dog's body like this. Tumors that keep growing, bleeding that will not stop, and a precarious state where infection could end the dog's life at any moment.

We can wait no longer. WeAct is going to rescue Gijeok.

WeAct does not name animals before they come into our arms.

But this time is different.

We named this dog first, hoping it would live no matter what.

'Gijeok' (Miracle).

A condition that is hard to explain medically, and a hope that the dog will survive even though the odds of survival are not high.

If even a 0.1% chance of saving this dog remains, we will take that path.

So that path can continue, donations for Gijeok's treatment are urgently needed.

Gijeok's time, seen through Road View.

Ten years, the time in which even mountains and rivers are said to change. For that long, there was a dog who kept to one spot without change.

Even as everything around changed, the dog was always in that place. It stayed there.

The dog's time seemed to stand still.

But even in time that had stopped, something kept growing steadily. A tumor, a devil inside the body, frighteningly diligent and never idle for a moment.

The tumors slowly consumed the dog's body.

Tumors were found all over the body. From the size of a fingernail to bigger than a fist. Too many to count.

What the dog needs now is a 'miracle.' Please look into the dog's eyes.

Eyes saying they want to live, asking for help.

If you send your donation with the depositor name <Gijeok> or <Gimhae tumor white dog>, it will be used entirely for Gijeok's treatment.

Please make a miracle happen.
