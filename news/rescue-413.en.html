Rescue news · 2026.08.05

Neglected dog in Guri

I am a retriever who has been abandoned all my life.

It is 39 degrees, too hot for even people to walk the street. I live in a small car in front of a villa.

Why? They say it is because I cannot climb the stairs. Dad says I am too heavy for him to carry me home.

Even when the heat leaves me gasping for breath, even when the cruel cold wind makes me shake all over, this is how I have to live.

I squeeze myself into the cramped car looking for shade and breathe a handful of air through a window opened just a crack, enduring this prison-like life. Only then will I not be abandoned again.

A life used to being abandoned. I have never had a proper family or home. I am a retriever who is nothing but a nuisance.

I have been abandoned all my life.

Three years at an apartment entrance, because my fur would fly around and I could not be let indoors.

After being abandoned with a note saying anyone could keep me, I was tied up outside, and then spent another 5 years in a cafe and a car.

Only yesterday did I leave the car that was like a boiling cauldron. Now I am at the hospital.

Everyone, please donate for me. I want to live properly, in a home instead of a car, as someone's family instead of a nuisance. I want to stop being pitiable, stop being unhappy. I really do not want to be abandoned anymore.

📍Please donate for the dog who was kept trapped in a small car during the heat wave.
If you send your donation with the depositor name [#RetrieverTrappedInCar] or [Retriever], it will be used entirely for the dog.
