Rescue news · 2026.09.19

Illegal new-style pet shop in Mapo

Over 14 hours, WeAct rescued a total of 21 dogs. All of the rescued dogs are now hospitalized and receiving tests and treatment.

The dogs were said to be "well looked after." We were told that the sick ones had been taken to a hospital and discharged after being told they were healthy.

But tests have already confirmed infections with coronavirus, giardia and campylobacter.

Some dogs were scratching constantly or losing fur, so skin diseases such as a mange mite infection are suspected. Many were coughing repeatedly, so additional tests for respiratory disease are under way.

Two operators were present at this rescue. One of them was a WeAct adopter.

After long persuasion, the two operators signed a statement giving up ownership of the 21 animals to be rescued.

The WeAct adopter also agreed not to run any business that profits from animals in the future.

WeAct will monitor whether these commitments are actually kept.

We rescued 21 dogs all at once with no warning, and highly contagious diseases are being confirmed, so we expect substantial veterinary costs ahead.

We urgently need your help with hospital bills so that the dogs can finish the treatment they need and regain their health.

We will share more about the rescue process, the dogs' health and the results of further tests as soon as they are ready.

Finally, at this site we reached some agreements with the operators in the course of putting a quick surrender of ownership and a safe handover of the dogs first. We ask that you refrain from identifying or publishing the name of the business or the real names of the people involved.
