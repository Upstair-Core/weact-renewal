Where smaller costs more, and where bigger costs more

Where smaller costs more, and where bigger costs more.

The same dog, but the price differs by size and by place?

In pet shops, small, cute dogs are sold at high prices.

But at dog meat auctions, it's different.

Heavy, fleshy Tosa dogs are classified as top grade. Smaller dogs, on the other hand, are classified as "for broth."

In one place, the smaller and cuter the better,

in the other, the bigger and fleshier the better.

The same life, yet the standard of pricing is the exact opposite depending on what humans want.

Depending on looks and build, humans give some the value of a companion and others the value of an ingredient.

Where smaller costs more, and where bigger costs more.

What changes is the price tag humans put on them. That price tag cannot decide the value of a life.
