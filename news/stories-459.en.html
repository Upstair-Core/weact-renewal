Life after rescue

WeAct monthly newsletter, September issue

Monthly WeAct, September issue

What does WeAct mean to you?
What might you mean to WeAct?

An account that suddenly appeared when you opened social media. People saving lives that looked about to die at any moment. You wonder why they bother, and then at some point, without realizing it, you keep looking in. As you kept looking, the sparkling names and faces must have begun to be etched in your hearts.

To WeAct, you are the people who make that sparkle. Without your cheers, we would have been only half the people we are.

In September, we had many feelings. People who buy and sell animals. And people who don't even know that's wrong, who call themselves experts and feel compassion only for themselves. We felt deep disappointment in humans, yet in turn we were comforted by you.

The 21 lives WeAct saved in September. It was because there was warmth in our hearts that this was not stained with despair and tears alone. Thank you so much for last month as well.
