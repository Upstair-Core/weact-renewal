Life after rescue

The dog who loved people most, the dog who was loneliest because of people

Gijeok is in a lot of... pain.

So please listen to Gijeok's story, even if it is a bit long.

Gijeok is a dog who loves people with all his heart.
Even though it was people who made Gijeok so terribly lonely.

Last April, Gijeok was rescued after ten years of neglect.
At the time of the rescue, a huge tumor hung from his neck, almost too big to believe.

Even with such a painful body, when someone reached out a hand,
he would lean his struggling body against it
as if he had waited his whole life for that one touch.

After the tumor hanging from his neck was removed,
we thought good days would finally begin for Gijeok.
Fresh from surgery, he pushed at the door with his whole body
to get out of his hospital pen,
and walked about bravely, wrapped in bandages.

Where in that struggling body was such strength hiding?
Back then we had no idea
we would miss the sight of Gijeok walking this much.

Little by little Gijeok lost the strength to walk,
and in the end could no longer stand on his own.

Even so, he was a tidy dog who gathered his remaining strength to crawl off the bed to relieve himself.
He even found a comfortable spot on his own, shifting his uncomfortable body this way and that.

But now he can't even do that.
Unless someone turns his body, he can't even change position,
and he relieves himself where he lies.

Gijeok, who once pushed at the door with his whole body,
now has no strength left even to turn himself over.

Yet today, too, Gijeok eats on his own.
When a person comes near, his big, clear eyes still sparkle.

Strength is leaving Gijeok's body one piece at a time,
but the look in his eyes as he gazes at people has not dimmed at all.

Looking into those eyes, we keep feeling how unfair it is.
After ten lonely years, he has only just begun to be loved.
Only now doesn't have to wait endlessly for someone's touch.

Why is even that ordinary happiness allowed to Gijeok for such a short time?

Please give Gijeok one minute of your time.
Call Gijeok's name.
Tell him you love him, that you love him very much.

So that Gijeok, who loves people so much,
can receive the biggest, most love in the world for the time he has left.

We ask this of you.

Our prettiest dog in the world, Gijeok.
We love you so much, our baby. 🤍
