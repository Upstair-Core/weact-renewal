Life after rescue

The adoption party everyone's been whispering about, revealed!

Is this really an adoption party? 👀 Wait, they do this at an adoption party? On November 7, the WeAct adoption party in Seongsu! It can't be only the dogs having fun, so we've prepared things for people to really play, too. 😎

🎰 Just pull one. No blanks! Give the gacha a spin before you go~! Just by coming to the adoption party you can take home a gift! Thanks to the generous support and cheers of our sponsors, this lucky draw's prize lineup is already something else! Bring a roomy bag! 😎

🎨 Custom goods, decorated your way! From T-shirts and tote bags decorated to your taste, to Shrinkles keyrings you draw and cut out yourself, totally your style!

🐶 My destiny dog is right here? The Destiny Dog Test, which might start as fun but get serious! We will tell you which dog at the adoption party is your perfect match.

Spinning the gacha, making goods, and collecting your gifts, we almost forgot the most important thing. The real stars of the day are the dogs! 🐶 Who knows whether a WeAct rescue dog you bump into while having fun might become someone's forever family?

Hoping for such unexpected encounters, we are holding the adoption party again this year. 💛 If you come to play and fall in love, we can't be held responsible! All proceeds raised from the events will go to the treatment and care of WeAct's rescued animals. 💗

🗓️ Saturday, November 7, 2026, 11:00–18:00 📍 Espace Cosy, Seongsu-dong (12 Wangsimni-ro 10-gil, Seongdong-gu, Seoul) Please check before you visit. ☑️ 🎟️ No reservation needed! Admission is FREE! 🐕 For safety, pets cannot be brought in. 🚗 Please use a nearby parking lot. It may be crowded on weekends, so we recommend public transportation! 🎁 Some events and activities may close early once the prepared quantities run out.
