Heim's sleepover diary

Heim's sleepover diary📝

"Teacher, why am I getting a bath?"

Out of nowhere, I got a scrubbing bath.
My turn has finally come!
Sleepover day with my teacher💛

Half excited, half nervous,
I set off for my teacher's home.
I firmly promised myself, "I'm going to play all I want."

Despite my promise, I was honestly a little scared at first...
The automatic door and the stairs were all unfamiliar,
and, embarrassingly, I even froze in fright at the sound of the dehumidifier.

But adapting is a piece of cake for me!
I nailed the walk on a busy commute,
and bravely hopped into the elevator!
The crunch of the fallen leaves sounded like cheering for me!

While I was at it, I went to a big shopping mall too.
Indoors, I listened to my teacher and was calm and quiet!
On the big playground, I ran with lots of energy!
Maybe I'm a shopping mall kind of dog...?

Now my teacher's home feels like my own.
Not wanting to just let the last night go by,
I played with toys and stuffed animals all night until dawn.

My friends who went on sleepovers bragged so much
that I was always jealous, but now I have something to brag about, too!
When I go back to the adoption center, I'm going to brag all I want!

"Guys! I went on a sleepover too!"
