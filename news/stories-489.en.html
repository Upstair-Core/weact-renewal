Love Pepero relay

Pe: Did you buy Pepero..?

Ro: they say your odds of winning the lottery go up! Hehe🍀

November 11 is Pepero Day,
the day we share sweet feelings with each other!

For the price of one snack,
why not give our rescue dogs the sweetest gift in the world?

Sweeter and more addictive than chocolate,
the dogs' adorable photos are your gift!

Thanks to everyone who shared a warm heart,
the dogs' day shines bright again today.

Save on a snack today and join our little relay
to share KRW 1,111 of warmth! 💝

(Murmur.. murmur..👥) Aunties and uncles who accidentally add a 1.. or a 0
to what they send are the best..!?🤭❤️

We will work hard so that the happiness of WeAct's dogs
lasts as long as a Pepero stick, on and on~!
