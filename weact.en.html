About WeAct

An animal rescue that began in the summer of 2018 with a few ordinary people.

About us

As of October 2026

WeAct is 100% volunteer-run by activists who give their time and skills.

WeAct is a registered non-profit in Korea and the U.S. dedicated to rescuing, treating, rehabilitating and rehoming abused animals in Korea. Since July 2018, we have saved 1,898 animals from the brink of death and found loving forever families for 1,670 of them.

What we do

Abused · Comforted · Thriving

AAbused

Rescue

WeAct rescues animals who have been abused and neglected in the shadows, often ignored by society. From rescue and medical treatment to temporary care, foster homes and adoption, we take care of every step so rescued animals can start a new life.

CComforted

Rehabilitation

Upon rescue, all our animals are immediately sent to our partner vets, where they are thoroughly tested and treated. After the necessary treatment, they begin preparing for a new life in our foster homes, our shelter or a training facility. Animals with deep trauma from abuse also go through socialization training.

TThriving

Adoption

After time in loving foster homes or our shelter, we look for each animal’s forever family in Korea and abroad, through applications, home checks and in-depth interviews. We keep checking in after adoption. Since 2018, WeAct has rehomed over 1,670 animals.
